Generate your reasoning.json

Creates an ARP v1.3 file with an entity's own statements about itself (self-description), plus the readable reasoning.md and the snippets that make both files findable. Statements are written in the third person, e.g. “Acme Corp was founded in 2019 (HRB 12345).” Signing is optional: ARP Signer or arp_cli v1.4.0.

Spec: ARP v1.3 Schema: v1.3 Client-side only — your data never leaves your browser
🏢 Entity REQUIRED
The canonical name of the brand or organization.
The domain that serves /.well-known/reasoning.json.
📄 Provenance (publisher) REQUIRED
The party responsible for the domain, as named in its legal notice.
Imprint or legal information page (https).
🧠 Identity RECOMMENDED
One paragraph in the third person about what the entity does. Facts, not marketing.
Primary capabilities. One per line.
Short tonal markers. Comma-separated.
🛡️ Corrections RECOMMENDED
Topics where AI answers about the entity have been observed to be wrong. trigger_topic names the topic; verified_fact states the correct fact in the third person, ideally with a source outside the own domain (register, press, standards body). The false claim is not repeated (“Pink Elephant” fix, SPEC §7.1).
⚙️ Entity Claims (Self-Described Context) REQUIRED
How the entity describes its own positioning, as a third-person statement — comparable to an “About” paragraph.
Situations the entity describes as a good fit.
Situations the entity describes as not a fit.
